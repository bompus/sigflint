# Release 1.0.4 pilots (not pooled with the campaign)

Two pilots ran before the campaign, through the same scripts and tooling, to cover each kind of case the campaign contains.
Neither is part of the campaign's results.

## Pilot 1: old timers against Tinybench and Hyperfine on the frozen artifact (parts A and D), 2026-10-31

Part D: four admitted jobs of three rounds each (seven cold starts per arm and runtime per round). Part A: one admitted pass of the old
hand-written timer (24 of 24 units) and one of Tinybench (22 of 24; round two of sigflint-A and aws4fetch used their four attempts on host
pressure), then a repeat for the cells that moved more than 10% (new runners 4 of 10 units, old timer round one 5 of 5). Results: 78 speed
cells differ by a median of 2.0% between the methods (71 within 10%); the cells that moved more than 10% were single-pass noise (the
old timer's second pass agreed with the new runner); cold-start figures agree within 1.5 ms. The table follows as generated by the
comparison script (`comparison.md`, old = hand-written batch timer, new = Tinybench or Hyperfine).

## Pilot 2: one unit of part B and one of part C with the calibration, 2026-11-01 (UTC), through run-admitted.sh with the freeze check

Part B, unit B-r1-aws4: admitted on the first measured attempt (the host lock was held by another session on two tries and the host was not
quiet on two more, none of which used an attempt); 30 rows, 28 ok and 2 wrong-result (rows where a library signs differently from the reference; they are
retained and not timed). Part C, units C-r1-sigflint-A and C-r1-s3mini: the allocation calibration ran between two admission checks (12
calibration rows); sigflint-A was admitted on its third attempt, s3mini on its first; 65 rows: 33 ok, 5 pending-log (Bun allocation
runs, whose GC logs become region rows), 12 unsupported (workloads s3mini cannot sign), and 15 Bun GC-region rows. The freeze check passed at the start of
each part ("frozen inputs match"). `tidy-results.mjs` dropped nothing. `analyze-memory.mjs` ran on the output; `analyze-more.mjs`
needs both sigflint controls, which the pilot's `ONLY` filter left out, and now says so instead of failing on an undefined value.
